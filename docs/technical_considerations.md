# **Hosting price summary — Render \+ 5 reputable options**

## **Render (will be used for staging)**  Render now structures its pricing primarily around user-based plans (Hobby, Professional, Organization, Enterprise) which include platform features and collaboration tools, plus separate compute and data service costs.

## Render User Plans (Platform & Collaboration)

1. ## Hobby: $0

## For hobbyists & students. Includes custom domains, global CDN, managed datastores, email support.

2. ## Professional: $19	

## For small teams & early-stage startups. Everything in Hobby, plus 500 GB bandwidth, up to 10 team members, preview/isolated environments, autoscaling, chat support.

3. ## Organization: $29

## For larger teams. Everything in Professional, plus 1 TB bandwidth, unlimited team members, audit logs, SOC 2 / ISO 27001 compliance.

4. ## Enterprise	Custom: Mission-critical applications. Includes SAML SSO, SCIM, guaranteed uptime, premium support, centralized management.

## Official Plan Pages: https://render.com/pricing
