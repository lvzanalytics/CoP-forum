# **Technical Proposal: Community of Practice Platform for Public Policy Evaluation**

*By LVZ Analytics Corp*

## **Executive Summary**

LVZ Analytics Corp proposes to establish a Community of Practice platform for public policy evaluation professionals in Côte d’Ivoire. Our approach combines community design, technology implementation, and capacity building over a three-month period.

**Three Integrated Components:** \- Component 1: Community structure and governance design \- Component 2: Interactive digital platform delivery \- Component 3: Training, documentation, and knowledge transfer

**Nine Key Deliverables:** 1\. Stakeholder needs analysis report 2\. Conceptual model and operational charter 3\. Community engagement plan with activities calendar 4\. Technology options analysis report 5\. Validated platform specifications 6\. Functional platform (tested and operational) 7\. User guide and technical documentation 8\. Training report and materials 9\. Maintenance and after-sales service plan

**Core Platform Features:** \- Discussion forums for professional exchange \- Documentation repository for reports and resources \- Member registration with differentiated access levels \- Integration with Zoom, Teams, and social networks

All documentation will be delivered in French. Ministry staff will be fully trained to manage the platform independently.

## **1\. Understanding the Assignment**

The public policy evaluation community in Côte d’Ivoire faces challenges including inconsistent practices, difficulty connecting quality evaluators with opportunities, and limited information sharing. Following Law No. 666 of December 14, 2022, the government has initiated measures to strengthen evaluation culture including a national evaluation policy, methodological guide, and evaluation certificate program.

The proposed Community of Practice platform addresses these challenges by creating a central space where evaluation professionals can connect, collaborate, and share knowledge. This assignment requires designing community structures and governance, selecting and implementing appropriate platform technology, and transferring knowledge to ensure the Ministry can independently manage and sustain the platform.

## **2\. Component 1: Community Structure and Governance Design**

### **2.1 Stakeholder Needs Assessment**

We will work with the Ministry to identify the functional and technical needs for the CoP, covering community objectives, types of interactions needed, content to be shared, and participation methods.

**Deliverable:** Stakeholder needs analysis report

### **2.2 Governance Architecture and Operational Charter**

We will propose governance structures including leadership roles (community managers, moderators, administrators), decision-making processes, and content management procedures. The operational charter will establish community mission, code of conduct, and member rights and responsibilities.

**Deliverables:** \- Memo outlining community structure and operations \- Conceptual model and operational charter

### **2.3 Community Engagement Plan**

We will develop a strategy for launching and sustaining the community, including a calendar of activities to maintain member engagement.

**Deliverable:** Community engagement plan with activities calendar

## **3\. Component 2: Interactive Platform Delivery**

### **3.1 Technology Research and Selection**

Upon contract award, we will conduct comprehensive research into technology solutions for the CoP platform. This research will examine both existing community platforms and custom development approaches to determine the optimal solution.

**Research Scope:**

We will document and analyze:

1. **Open Source Community Platforms** \- Proven forum and collaboration platforms used by professional communities globally (examples include Discourse, NodeBB, and similar solutions)

2. **Specialized Community of Practice Software** \- Purpose-built tools designed specifically for communities of practice and professional learning networks

3. **Custom Development Approaches** \- Modern web application frameworks suitable for building a tailored solution from scratch

**Evaluation Criteria:**

For each option, we will assess:

| Criteria | What We’ll Assess |
| :---- | :---- |
| Feature completeness | Does it support discussions, document sharing, member management, integrations? Can it be extended to meet specific needs? |
| Development effort | How much work to implement the required features within 3 months? |
| Maintenance burden | How much technical expertise required for ongoing operations? |
| Local capacity fit | Can Ministry IT staff manage it after handover? Are the required skills available locally in Côte d’Ivoire? |
| Mobile experience | Does it work well on phones and connectivity conditions common in Côte d’Ivoire? |
| Integration capability | How easily can we connect Zoom, Teams, social networks? |
| Total cost of ownership | Initial development \+ ongoing hosting, maintenance, and support costs |
| Scalability | Can it grow with the community over time? |

**Technology Approach:**

Based on our research findings, we will recommend one of two approaches:

1. **Adapt an Existing Platform** \- Select a proven open source community platform, customize it for the evaluation community’s needs, develop additional features as required, and deploy on Ministry-controlled infrastructure.

* *Typical advantages:* Faster deployment, proven stability, existing documentation and support resources

* *Typical considerations:* Working within platform architecture, maintaining compatibility with platform updates

2. **Build a Purpose-Built Solution** \- Develop a custom platform using modern web frameworks, designed specifically for the evaluation community’s workflows and requirements.

* *Typical advantages:* Complete control over features and user experience, no external dependencies, tailored to exact specifications

* *Considerations:* Comprehensive development and testing required, Ministry assumes full maintenance responsibility

**Selection Process:**

Our technology options analysis report will: \- Document all solutions researched with their capabilities and limitations \- Present a comparative analysis using the criteria above \- Provide a clear recommendation with detailed rationale \- Address how the recommended approach fits the 3-month timeline, Ministry technical capacity, and budget constraints \- Outline deployment approach on Ministry-owned infrastructure (Ministry servers or cloud hosting under Ministry control)

The platform will be delivered in French. All code, data, and content will be owned by the Ministry.

**Deliverable:** Technology options analysis report with recommendation and rationale

### **3.2 Platform Specifications and Features**

Once the technology approach is selected, we will develop detailed specifications. Here’s what the platform will include:

**Discussion Forums**

The forum will be organized into categories for different evaluation topics. Features include:

* **Topic Creation:** Any registered member can start a discussion with title and tags

* **Threaded Conversations:** Replies show who responded to whom

* **Rich Content:** Members can format text (bold, italics, lists), embed images, attach files, include links

* **Reactions:** Members can like posts or mark as helpful

* **Notifications:** Members receive email notifications for topics they’re following

**Documentation Repository**

A structured library for sharing evaluation reports, methodological guides, and resources:

* **Organized Categories:** Documents sorted by type (reports, tools, case studies), sector, region

* **Upload Process:** Upload files (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint)

* **Metadata:** Each document includes title, author, date, description, tags

* **Search:** Search across document titles, descriptions, and tags

* **Access Control:** Some documents can be public while others require membership

**Member Management System**

**Registration & Profiles:** \- Simple registration with email verification \- Member profiles include name, organization, expertise areas, location, bio, professional interests \- Optional profile photo \- Privacy controls \- members choose what information is visible to others

**Access Levels:**

| Level | Can Do | Example Use Case |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Public Visitor | View public discussions and documents, browse member directory | Sponsors looking for evaluators |
| Registered Member | Participate in discussions, upload documents, connect with members | Evaluation practitioners |
| Moderator | All member privileges plus: edit/delete inappropriate content, move topics to correct categories, pin important discussions | Experienced evaluators helping manage community |
| Administrator | Full platform control: manage users, configure settings, manage categories | Ministry staff |

**Member Directory:** \- Search by expertise (e.g., find all evaluators with health sector experience) \- Filter by location (find evaluators in specific regions) \- Filter by organization type (consultants, government, NGO, academic) \- Contact members directly through the platform

**Integration Capabilities**

**Videoconferencing (Zoom/Teams):** \- Create event posts that include Zoom/Teams meeting links \- Display upcoming events on the platform

**Social Media Sharing:** \- Share discussions to Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter

**Email System:** \- Email notifications for platform activity \- Members can control notification frequency

**Security & Data Protection**

* **Authentication:** Secure password storage with modern encryption

* **Password Requirements:** Minimum 8 characters

* **Account Recovery:** Password reset via email

* **Session Management:** Automatic logout after inactivity

* **HTTPS:** All data transmitted encrypted

* **Backups:** Daily automated backups of database and uploaded files

* **Spam Protection:** Basic protections against spam registrations

* **Data Privacy:** Members can delete their accounts and data

**Mobile Experience**

The platform will work on mobile devices without requiring an app download: \- Responsive design adjusts to screen size \- Touch-friendly navigation \- Readable text without zooming

**Optional Feature: AI-Powered Summarization**

If desired, we can integrate AI capabilities to help members stay on top of community activity:

* **Thread Summarization:** Automatically generate summaries of long discussion threads, highlighting key points and conclusions

* **Document Summarization:** Provide brief overviews of uploaded evaluation reports and documents

* **Catch-Up Summaries:** Help members who’ve been away understand what they missed

*Implementation considerations:* \- Uses established AI services (OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar) \- Requires ongoing API costs (estimated based on usage volume) \- Adds 1-2 weeks to development timeline \- Requires API key management and monitoring

This feature is optional and can be discussed based on budget and priorities.

**Deliverable:** Validated platform specifications document

### **3.3 Platform Development Process**

Our development approach will either adapt an existing open source platform or build a custom solution from scratch. Either way, we’re doing significant development work to create a platform tailored to the evaluation community’s needs.

**Weeks 1-2: Technology Selection and Foundation** Complete technology options analysis and get approval on approach. Set up development environment, hosting infrastructure, and database structure. Implement core authentication and security measures.

**Weeks 3-7: Core Development** Build all platform features: discussion forums with categories and moderation tools, documentation repository with upload and search, member registration and profile system with four access levels, French language interface, integrations with Zoom/Teams and social networks, email notification system, and administrative dashboard.

**Weeks 8-9: Testing and Refinement** Test all features across devices and browsers. Ministry staff conduct user acceptance testing. Fix issues and refine based on feedback.

**Deliverable:** Functional platform, tested and ready for deployment

### **3.4 Documentation and Maintenance Planning**

**User Guide:**

The user guide will explain in French with screenshots: \- How to register and create a profile \- Participating in discussions \- Sharing documents and resources \- Finding and connecting with other members

**Administrator Guide:**

The administrator guide will cover: \- Approving member registrations \- Assigning roles and permissions \- Moderating discussions and content \- Managing the documentation repository \- Platform configuration and settings \- Backup and restore procedures \- Troubleshooting common issues

**Video Tutorials:**

Short videos demonstrating: \- Key user tasks (registration, discussion participation) \- Administrator functions (content moderation, user management) \- Maintenance procedures (backups)

**Maintenance Plan:**

The maintenance plan will define ongoing platform sustainability covering: \- Bug fixes and technical issue resolution procedures \- Security updates and patches process \- Software updates and upgrades schedule \- Hosting arrangements and server management \- Backup and disaster recovery procedures \- Technical support contact and response times \- Ongoing costs and budget requirements

**Deliverable:** User guide, administrator guide, video tutorials, and maintenance plan

## **4\. Component 3: Implementation Support and Knowledge Transfer**

### **4.1 Training Program**

We will deliver training in French for ministry staff to manage the platform independently. Training will be hands-on with practice using the actual platform.

**Training Coverage:** \- Using platform features (discussions, document sharing, member directory) \- Moderating discussions and managing content \- Approving member registrations and assigning roles \- Basic platform configuration and settings \- Backup procedures and when to contact technical support

**Training Delivery:** \- Hands-on sessions with the actual platform \- Training materials in French

**Deliverable:** Training report and training materials

### **4.2 Initial Support and Knowledge Transfer**

During the first month of platform operation, we will provide support as ministry staff gain practical experience managing the platform.

**Support Activities:** \- Available via email and video calls for questions \- Help resolving technical issues \- Guidance on handling community management scenarios \- Progressive handover of responsibilities as staff demonstrate capability

**Knowledge Transfer Process:** 1\. Staff manage routine tasks (approving registrations, monitoring discussions) with our close support 2\. Staff take on more complex tasks (moderating difficult situations, technical configurations) as they gain confidence 3\. Document any custom procedures or configurations 4\. Transfer all technical credentials and access information 5\. Final handover with staff managing independently

**Deliverable:** Training materials and support documentation

## **5\. Implementation Timeline**

The project will be delivered over 12 weeks (3 months) from December 2025 through February 2026\.

**Weeks 1-2: Foundation** \- Identify functional and technical needs for the CoP \- Technology options analysis and recommendation \- Begin governance architecture and charter development \- Set up development environment and hosting infrastructure

**Weeks 3-7: Platform Development** \- Finalize platform specifications \- Build all platform features (forums, documentation repository, member management, integrations) \- Develop French language interface \- Implement security measures and administrative controls \- Develop community engagement plan and activities calendar

**Weeks 8-9: Testing** \- Test all features across devices and browsers \- Ministry staff conduct user acceptance testing \- Fix issues and refine based on feedback

**Weeks 10-11: Documentation and Training** \- Prepare user guide, administrator guide, and video tutorials \- Finalize maintenance plan \- Deliver training to ministry staff \- Platform launch

**Week 12: Handover** \- Initial support as ministry staff manage live platform \- Transfer all credentials and documentation \- Final handover

**All Deliverables Completed:** Needs analysis, governance documents, engagement plan, technology analysis, specifications, functional platform, documentation, training materials, maintenance plan
